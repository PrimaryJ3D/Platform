FAQ

Questions delegates ask.

How exclusive is the House of Collaboration?

Invitation-only. Every delegate is vetted. Around 850 senior leaders take part in each edition.

Do you have partnership pricing?

Yes - Entry, Mid and High tiers. Request the prospectus or book a call and we’ll match you to the right one.

What’s included in each tier?

Entry = brand presence + ROI report. Mid = Entry + active participation + curated matchmaking. High = Mid + full strategic partnership + agenda-driven access.

Can I send a deputy?

In limited circumstances. Cohort integrity is core to the format, so we keep substitutions to a minimum.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes. The Chatham House Rule applies inside all closed sessions.

How is HoC different from a typical Davos event?

Decision density over audience size. Curated invitation, not paid access. Long-term capital and policy deployment, not short-term deal flow.

When are sector-exclusive allocations confirmed?

On a rolling basis. Sectors close as commitments come in, so earlier conversations have more room.

How do I apply?

Apply via the Airtable form, or book a 30-minute partnership call.

Davos 2025 - Peace as Policy.

The second edition of the House of Collaboration. 150 delegates. Foresight as practice. Technology with purpose.

2025 Theme

Peace as Policy. Foresight as Practice. Technology with Purpose.

The 2025 edition convened 150 delegates from across the world during Davos week. The week was anchored on three propositions: that peace is a policy choice, foresight is a practice rather than a forecast, and technology serves human ends or it serves none.

Highlights included the launch of the Foresight Whitepaper (42 experts, 9 visions of the future), the inaugural Estonian Leap programme, and the first Peace Gala Dinner during Davos week.

Inside Davos Foresight Whitepaper - Our World in 2125.

Methodology

Foresight as practice, not prediction.

The Foresight Whitepaper is a structured contribution from 42 global experts - founders, scientists, policymakers, philosophers and capital allocators - each providing a sectoral 100-year forecast under shared methodological constraints. The result is nine plausible visions of our world in 2125, grouped across thematic clusters.

Co-developed with the Bavarian Foresight Institute, the document was distributed in print at HoC Davos 2025 to heads of state, sovereign wealth funds, and senior multilateral leaders.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following GDPR Article 6 bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) - for marketing communications, optional cookies, newsletter sign-ups
  • Contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)) - for event participation, advisory engagements, service delivery
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) - for security logging, fraud prevention, and improvement of our services where these interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) - for invoicing, tax records, and statutory retention

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use the following categories of cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Strictly necessary - session management, security, form submission. No consent required.
  • Analytics - aggregate visitor behaviour to improve content. We use Google Analytics 4 in IP-anonymised mode with Consent Mode v2. Loaded only after consent.
  • Marketing - HubSpot tracking pixel for lead attribution and campaign measurement. Loaded only after consent.

You may withdraw consent at any time via the cookie banner or by emailing primary@j3d.ai. Refusing non-essential cookies will not impair your access to the site.

5. Third-Party Processors

  • HubSpot Ireland Limited - CRM, forms, marketing automation (EU data centre)
  • Calendly LLC - meeting booking (US, SCCs in place)
  • Google LLC - Workspace email and analytics (EU data residency where available)
  • Microsoft Azure - legacy hosting for davos.j3d.ai archive (EU region)

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) - obtain a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) - correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure / “right to be forgotten” (Art. 17)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20)
  • Right to object (Art. 21) - in particular against direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Estonia: Andmekaitse Inspektsioon)

To exercise any of these rights, email primary@j3d.ai. We respond within 30 days.
